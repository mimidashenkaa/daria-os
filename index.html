SKVOT

Building identity from naming to colour, typography, composition and logo systems.

Graphic Design & Visual Communication
Apr — Jun 2026
Constantin Șarcov
Identity · Type · Composition

Identity as a system, not just a logo.

During SKVOT's Graphic Design & Visual Communication course, I worked through the foundations of building a visual identity — starting with fictional concepts and gradually developing naming, colour, typography, composition and logo explorations.

The process was developed through recurring assignments and curator feedback, allowing each stage to inform the next.

00 / Process

From naming
to a visual world.

Two fictional organisations were selected and developed through successive identity exercises.

01 / Institutul pentru Haos Bine Intenționat

Well-intentioned
chaos.

A visual identity built around the tension between structure and disorder — organised typography interrupted by expressive scribbles and unpredictable composition.

02 / Aspect Ratio 2.39:1

Living inside
the frame.

The visual language turns the widescreen ratio itself into a graphic device, using stretched typography, compressed space and cinema-like framing.

Outcome

The course changed the way I approach identity work: less as a final logo and more as a connected system of decisions across typography, colour, composition and application.
